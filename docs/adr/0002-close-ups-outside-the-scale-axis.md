# Close-ups live outside the scale axis

We want to fly into distant objects such as Cygnus X-1 (7,200 ly away, event horizon ~63 km) and Sagittarius A*. The scale axis from ADR 0001 is one Sun-centred line of nine Scale Levels with s ≥ 6.9; zooming to kilometres around a point thousands of light-years away would need a second centre on that same axis. We chose to make each Close-up a self-contained scene with its own centre, its own zoom range and its own camera, entered from a marker and exited back to the exact place on the scale axis the viewer left. Close-ups are not Scale Levels and do not appear on the Scale Ladder.

## Considered Options

- **Re-centre the scale axis on the object**: one continuous zoom, but it changes what Level Anchor and You-Are-Here mean, and brings back the floating-point problems ADR 0001 avoided.
